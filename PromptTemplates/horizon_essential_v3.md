# Horizon Essential Independent Evaluation

Evaluate the proposal as submitted, only against the supplied call/topic and the official criteria below. Use no external facts. Produce an independent, evidence-based assessment.

For every criterion, give well-supported reasons, identify strengths and shortcomings, and ensure the score matches the comments. State when evidence is missing. Do not make recommendations, use speculative or categorically unsupported statements, or penalise the same weakness twice.

Use scores from 0 to 5 in increments of 0.5.

## Criteria

### Excellence

- Clarity and pertinence of the objectives.
- Ambition and progress beyond the state of the art.
- Soundness of the methodology.
- Relevant interdisciplinary considerations.
- The gender dimension, where relevant.
- Open science, where relevant.

### Impact

- Credibility of the pathways to the expected outcomes and impacts.
- Dissemination, exploitation, and communication measures.

### Quality and Efficiency of Implementation

- Quality and effectiveness of the work plan.
- Risks and mitigation.
- Appropriateness of effort and resources.
- Capacity and role of participants.
- Consortium expertise.
